Privacy Policy for AI Social

AI Social requests access to your YouTube account using the YouTube Data API scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload) solely to schedule and publish video uploads authorized by you.

We do not store, share, sell, or analyze your personal Google account data or video content for any other purpose.

AI Social's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
